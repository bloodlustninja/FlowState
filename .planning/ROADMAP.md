# FlowState Roadmap

## Overview
This roadmap tracks the development of the FlowState precision racer across core physics, input, shaders, traffic choreography, and arcade scoring polish.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Physics Core & Vehicle Rig (Completed)
- **Goal**: Implement self-contained 4-ray hover spring vehicle with artificial downforce, surface alignment, cruise thrust, and lateral grip/drift toggle.

### Phase 2: Single-Thumb Input & Follow Camera (Completed)
- **Goal**: Implement touch gesture recognition (steering delta, downward flick drift trigger) and the follow camera with rotational lag and look-ahead.

### Phase 3: Comic Shaders & Visual Shell (Completed)
- **Goal**: Implement zero-asset procedural meshes, Moebius/Ultimate Spider-Man palette, inverted-hull outlines, and screen-space crosshatching toon shaders.

### Phase 4: Choreographed Traffic & Near-Miss Detection (Completed)
- **Goal**: Implement traffic choreography (Zipper/Funnel), near-miss grazed time dilation, and sub-300ms crash-restart game loop.

### Phase 5: Auto Modellista Manga Visuals, Scoring Engine & Dynamic Traffic AI (Completed)
- **Goal**: Implement expanded arcade scoring engine with 2.5s combo multipliers (up to 4x), 0.6m graze detection, 0.035s hitstop microfreeze, dynamic civilian traffic AI with 1.2s lane-switching and 4° tilt, Auto Modellista manga cel-shading with warm cream paper tone (#EAE6DC) and cobalt racing stripes, unshaded red taillights (#FF1E27), edge-only manga speedlines, two-tone deep blue/cream sky, and comprehensive HUD summary stats.
